# Fish Tank Simulator

A Roblox game built as a solo-developer, self-sustaining business project: cast a line, catch fish, watch them grow in your own tank (even offline), sell or show them off, and upgrade your gear to catch rarer fish.

- **Start here:** [`PROJECT_STATE.md`](PROJECT_STATE.md) — full project handoff/summary.
- **Design & business docs:** [`docs/`](docs/) — market research, concept scoring, game design document, monetization/revenue model, technical architecture, testing plan, presentation copy, and launch checklist, in that order.
- **Game code:** [`src/`](src/) — a [Rojo](https://rojo.space/) project. See `docs/09_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` step 1 for exact sync instructions into Roblox Studio.

No external servers, paid APIs, or recurring infrastructure costs — the entire game runs on Roblox's own free, first-party services (DataStore, MarketplaceService, Analytics).
